# Dress Code Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All employees, based on role and work location

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets expectations for professional appearance at Apex Global Technologies, balancing a comfortable work environment with a professional image for clients, partners, and visitors.

## 2. General Standard

Employees are expected to dress in a manner that is clean, neat, and appropriate for a professional business environment, taking into account their specific role and whether they interact with clients or the public.

## 3. Business Casual (Standard Office Days)

- Collared shirts, blouses, sweaters, or smart tops
- Slacks, chinos, skirts, or tailored dresses
- Closed-toe shoes or smart casual footwear
- Avoid: overly casual items such as gym wear, beachwear, or clothing with offensive graphics/text

## 4. Business Formal (Client-Facing Days, Meetings, Events)

- Suits, blazers, or formal dresses
- Formal shoes
- Minimal, professional accessories

## 5. Casual/Dress-Down Days (if applicable)

On designated casual days (e.g., Fridays), smart jeans and casual shirts/tops are acceptable, provided they remain neat and appropriate; ripped, torn, or overly worn clothing is not permitted.

## 6. Role-Specific Requirements

- Employees working in data centers, warehouses, or technical/field environments must follow role-specific safety and PPE requirements, which take precedence over general dress code standards.
- Client-facing and customer service staff may be required to wear company-branded attire or uniforms where provided.

## 7. Remote/Video Call Standard

Employees working remotely should maintain a professional appearance for video calls consistent with the business casual standard, particularly for client-facing or external meetings.

## 8. Religious and Medical Accommodations

The company will make reasonable accommodations to this policy for religious, medical, or disability-related needs; employees should raise such requests with HR.

## 9. Non-Compliance

Employees who do not meet dress code expectations may be asked to change or, for repeated non-compliance, may be subject to a documented conversation with their manager or HR.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed periodically and may be adjusted based on business needs, client feedback, or office culture changes.
