# Code of Conduct

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and representatives of the company

## 1. Purpose

This Code of Conduct sets out the standards of behavior expected of everyone at Apex Global Technologies, to maintain a respectful, safe, and productive workplace, and to protect the company's reputation and integrity.

## 2. Core Principles

- **Integrity:** Act honestly and ethically in all business dealings.
- **Respect:** Treat colleagues, customers, and partners with courtesy and respect, regardless of position or background.
- **Accountability:** Take ownership of your work, decisions, and mistakes.
- **Compliance:** Follow all applicable laws, regulations, and company policies.

## 3. Professional Behavior

- Employees are expected to perform their duties diligently, arrive on time, and meet agreed commitments.
- Disagreements should be handled professionally and constructively, escalated through appropriate channels when needed.
- Use of abusive, threatening, or discriminatory language is not tolerated.

## 4. Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must disclose any personal, financial, or business relationship that could reasonably be seen to conflict with the company's interests.
- Employees must not use their position for personal gain at the company's expense.
- Gifts or hospitality from vendors/clients beyond nominal value must be declared to management.

## 5. Confidentiality

- Employees must protect confidential company, customer, and colleague information, both during and after employment.
- Confidential information must not be disclosed to unauthorized parties or used for personal benefit.

## 6. Anti-Bribery and Corruption

Employees must not offer, give, solicit, or accept bribes or improper payments in connection with company business, in compliance with applicable anti-corruption laws.

## 7. Workplace Behavior

- Harassment, bullying, discrimination, or victimization of any kind will not be tolerated (see the Anti-Harassment and Equal Opportunity Policy for full detail).
- Employees must not attend work under the influence of alcohol or illegal substances.
- Company property and resources must be used responsibly and primarily for business purposes.

## 8. Social Media and Public Representation

- Employees should not disclose confidential company information on personal social media.
- When identifying as a company employee publicly, individuals should make clear that views expressed are their own, not the company's, unless officially authorized to speak on its behalf.

## 9. Reporting Violations

Employees who become aware of a violation of this Code should report it to their manager, HR, or via the confidential whistleblowing channel, without fear of retaliation.

## 10. Consequences of Violation

Breach of this Code of Conduct may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to relevant authorities where unlawful conduct is involved.

## 11. Policy Review

This Code will be reviewed at least annually.

---
*Acknowledgement: All employees are required to read, understand, and sign acknowledgement of this Code of Conduct upon joining and annually thereafter.*
