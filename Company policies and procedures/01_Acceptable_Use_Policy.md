# Acceptable Use Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** IT Department
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and third parties with access to company systems

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines acceptable use of Apex Global Technologies' IT systems, devices, networks, and data, to protect the organization, its employees, and its customers from risks including malware, data breaches, legal liability, and reputational damage.

## 2. Scope

This policy applies to all company-owned and personally owned devices used to access company resources, including computers, mobile devices, email, internet access, cloud services (e.g., Microsoft 365), and internal applications.

## 3. General Use

- Company systems are provided primarily for business purposes. Limited, reasonable personal use is permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, consume excessive resources, or violate this policy.
- Users must not use company systems to engage in illegal activity, harassment, discrimination, or the creation/distribution of offensive material.
- Users are responsible for safeguarding their login credentials and must not share accounts or passwords with others.

## 4. Email and Communication

- Company email must not be used to send confidential information to unauthorized recipients.
- Users must exercise caution with attachments and links from unknown senders and report suspected phishing to IT immediately.
- Auto-forwarding of company email to personal/external accounts is prohibited unless approved by IT.

## 5. Internet Use

- Internet access is provided for business purposes. Access to illegal, offensive, or 
prohibited non-business websites, including gambling and pirated-content websites, is prohibited
- Downloading unauthorized or unlicensed software is prohibited.

## 6. Software and Hardware

- Only software approved and/or deployed by IT may be installed on company devices.
- Users must not disable security software (antivirus, endpoint protection, firewall) on any company device.
- Company equipment must be returned in good condition upon termination of employment or contract.

## 7. Data Protection

- Confidential and personal data must only be stored in approved company systems (e.g., SharePoint, OneDrive for Business), not personal cloud storage or removable media, unless explicitly authorized.
- Users must lock their workstation when unattended and follow a clean-desk approach for sensitive documents.

## 8. Monitoring

Apex Global Technologies reserves the right to monitor use of its systems, including email, internet activity, and device usage, to the extent permitted by applicable law, for security, compliance, and operational purposes.

## 9. Violations

Violation of this policy may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, and may be reported to law enforcement where illegal activity is involved.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually and updated as needed to reflect changes in technology, regulation, or business requirements.

---
*Acknowledgement: By using company systems, users confirm they have read, understood, and agree to comply with this Acceptable Use Policy.*
