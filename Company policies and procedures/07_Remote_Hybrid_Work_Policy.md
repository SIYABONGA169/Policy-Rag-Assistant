# Remote and Hybrid Work Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All eligible employees

## 1. Purpose

This policy sets out the terms under which employees may work remotely or in a hybrid arrangement (partly remote, partly on company premises), balancing flexibility with business needs and security requirements.

## 2. Eligibility

- Remote/hybrid work arrangements are available to roles where job duties can be effectively performed outside the office, subject to manager approval.
- Eligibility will be assessed based on role requirements, performance, and business need, not as an automatic entitlement.

## 3. Work Arrangement Options

- **Fully remote:** Employee works from an approved remote location on all scheduled working days.
- **Hybrid:** Employee splits time between the office and a remote location, per a schedule agreed with their manager (e.g., minimum **2 days** in-office per week).
- **Fully on-site:** Required for roles where physical presence is necessary (e.g., hardware/data center work).

## 4. Expectations While Working Remotely

- Employees must be reachable during agreed working hours via company communication tools (email, Teams, phone).
- Core working hours and response-time expectations are the same as when working on-site.
- Employees must attend required in-person meetings, training, or events when requested, with reasonable notice.

## 5. Workspace and Equipment

- Employees must maintain a safe, quiet, and professional workspace suitable for video calls and focused work.
- Company-issued equipment must be used in accordance with the Acceptable Use Policy, and must be kept secure at all times (e.g., not left unattended in public places).
- Requests for home office equipment (monitor, chair, etc.) should be submitted through IT procurement, subject to budget approval.

## 6. Security Requirements

- Remote work requires a secure, private internet connection; use of public Wi-Fi without VPN is discouraged.
- All company data access must comply with the Remote Access and VPN Policy and the Acceptable Use Policy.
- Confidential conversations and documents must be protected from being seen/heard by others sharing the remote workspace.

## 7. Performance Management

- Remote/hybrid employees are evaluated on the same performance standards and output expectations as on-site employees.
- Managers and employees should agree on clear deliverables and check-in cadence to maintain visibility and accountability.

## 8. Changes to Arrangement

- The company reserves the right to modify or revoke remote/hybrid arrangements based on business needs, with reasonable notice where possible.
- Employees relocating to a new address while working remotely must notify HR in advance, as this may affect tax, legal, or equipment logistics.

## 9. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually to reflect business needs and employee feedback.
