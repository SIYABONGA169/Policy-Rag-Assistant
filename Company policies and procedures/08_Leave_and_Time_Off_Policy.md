# Leave and Time Off Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All employees (full-time/part-time distinctions noted where applicable)

## 1. Purpose

This policy outlines the types of leave available to employees, eligibility, and the process for requesting time off, in line with applicable labor law.

## 2. Annual Leave

- Full-time employees accrue **21 consecutive days** (or 15 working days / 1 day per 17 days worked) of annual leave per year, pro-rated for part-time or mid-year joiners in accordance with BCEA.
- Leave requests should be submitted at least **7 business days** in advance via HR portal, subject to manager approval and business needs.
- Unused leave may be carried over up to a maximum of **5 days** into the next leave cycle, subject to local law and company discretion.

## 3. Sick Leave

- Employees are entitled to **30 working days** of paid sick leave per 36-month sick leave cycle, in accordance with BCEA.
- A medical certificate may be required for absences exceeding **2 consecutive days**.
- Employees should notify their manager as early as possible on the day of absence.

## 4. Public Holidays

Employees are entitled to all gazetted public holidays applicable in their work location. Where work is required on a public holiday, compensation or time off in lieu will be provided per local law and company policy.

## 5. Parental Leave

- Maternity Leave: 4 consecutive months unpaid/UIF in accordance with BCEA.
- Parental Leave: 10 consecutive days unpaid/UIF in accordance with BCEA.
- Employees should notify HR as early as practicable to allow for planning and handover arrangements.

## 6. Compassionate / Bereavement Leave

Employees may take up to **3 days** of paid family responsibility leave per 12-month cycle for full-time employees working 4+ days/week after 4 months, in the event of the death of an immediate family member, with additional unpaid leave available by arrangement if needed.

## 7. Unpaid Leave

Requests for extended unpaid leave will be considered on a case-by-case basis, subject to manager and HR approval, and business requirements.

## 8. Study Leave

Employees pursuing approved, job-relevant further education may apply for study leave for examinations, subject to manager approval and supporting documentation.

## 9. Requesting Leave

1. Submit a leave request via HR portal with as much advance notice as possible.
2. Manager reviews and approves/declines based on team coverage and business needs.
3. Approved leave is recorded in the HR system and payroll adjusted accordingly.

## 10. Abuse of Leave Policy

Falsifying reasons for leave, or repeated unauthorized absence, may result in disciplinary action.

## 11. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually and whenever local labor law changes affect leave entitlements.
