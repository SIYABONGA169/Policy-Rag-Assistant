# Security Incident Response Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** IT Security
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, and third parties

## 1. Purpose

This policy defines how Apex Global Technologies detects, responds to, and recovers from information security incidents, to limit damage, reduce recovery time, and meet legal/regulatory obligations.

## 2. Definition of a Security Incident

Any event that compromises the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of company systems or data, including but not limited to: malware/ransomware infection, unauthorized access, data breach, phishing compromise, denial-of-service attack, or loss/theft of a device containing company data.

## 3. Reporting an Incident

- Any employee who suspects a security incident must report it immediately to IT Security via security@apexglobal.co.za or the IT helpdesk, regardless of time of day.
- Do not attempt to investigate, "fix," or power off affected systems yourself unless instructed by IT Security, as this can destroy forensic evidence.
- When in doubt, report it — there is no penalty for reporting a false alarm in good faith.

## 4. Incident Response Team

A designated Incident Response Team (IRT), comprising IT Security, IT Operations, Legal/Compliance, and relevant business leadership, will be activated for any confirmed incident above a defined severity threshold.

## 5. Incident Response Phases

1. **Identification** – Confirm whether an event is a genuine security incident and classify its severity.
2. **Containment** – Isolate affected systems/accounts to prevent further spread (e.g., disable compromised accounts, isolate infected devices from the network).
3. **Eradication** – Remove the root cause (malware, unauthorized access, vulnerability).
4. **Recovery** – Restore affected systems from clean backups and return to normal operations, with enhanced monitoring.
5. **Lessons Learned** – Conduct a post-incident review within **7 business days** to document root cause, response effectiveness, and remediation actions.

## 6. Severity Classification

| Level | Description | Example |
|---|---|---|
| Critical | Widespread outage or active data breach | Ransomware across multiple systems |
| High | Significant but contained impact | Single compromised admin account |
| Medium | Limited impact, no data loss | Isolated malware on one device |
| Low | Minimal/no operational impact | Blocked phishing attempt |

## 7. Communication

- Internal communication during an incident will be coordinated by the IRT lead to avoid conflicting information.
- External communication (customers, regulators, media) must be approved by Legal/Compliance and senior management before release.
- Where personal data is affected, applicable data breach notification laws and timelines (e.g., POPIA, GDPR) must be followed.

## 8. Evidence Preservation

Logs, images of affected systems, and other evidence must be preserved in a forensically sound manner to support investigation and any potential legal action.

## 9. Roles and Responsibilities

- **All staff:** report suspected incidents promptly.
- **IT Security:** lead technical investigation, containment, and eradication.
- **IT Operations:** support recovery and system restoration.
- **Legal/Compliance:** advise on regulatory/notification obligations.
- **Management:** approve external communications and resourcing decisions.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy and the associated incident response plan will be tested via tabletop exercises at least annually and reviewed after every significant incident.
