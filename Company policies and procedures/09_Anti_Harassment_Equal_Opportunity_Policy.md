# Anti-Harassment and Equal Opportunity Policy

**Company:** Apex Global Technologies
**Effective Date:** 22 October 2026
**Policy Owner:** Human Resources
**Applies To:** All employees, contractors, job applicants, and visitors to company premises

## 1. Purpose

Apex Global Technologies is committed to providing a workplace free from harassment, discrimination, and victimization, and to ensuring equal opportunity in all aspects of employment.

## 2. Equal Opportunity Commitment

The company does not discriminate in hiring, promotion, compensation, training, or any other aspect of employment on the basis of race, color, gender, age, disability, religion, national origin, marital status, pregnancy, or any other characteristic protected by applicable law.

## 3. Definition of Harassment

Harassment includes any unwelcome conduct, verbal, physical, or visual, that creates an intimidating, hostile, or offensive work environment, or that unreasonably interferes with an individual's work performance. This includes, but is not limited to:

- Unwelcome sexual advances, comments, or physical contact
- Offensive jokes, slurs, or comments related to a protected characteristic
- Display of offensive materials (physical or digital)
- Bullying, intimidation, or persistent unreasonable criticism intended to demean

## 4. Victimization and Retaliation

Retaliation against anyone who reports harassment or discrimination in good faith, or who participates in an investigation, is strictly prohibited and will itself be treated as a disciplinary matter.

## 5. Reporting Procedure

1. Employees experiencing or witnessing harassment or discrimination should report it to their manager, HR, or via the confidential reporting channel.
2. Reports will be treated confidentially, with information shared only on a need-to-know basis for investigation purposes.
3. An investigation will be initiated promptly, typically within **48 hours** of a report being received, and completed within **7 business days** in accordance with EEA 2022 Code of Good Practice.

## 6. Investigation Process

- Investigations will be conducted impartially by HR or a designated investigator.
- Both the complainant and respondent will have an opportunity to present their account.
- Findings and any resulting action will be communicated to relevant parties, within the bounds of confidentiality.

## 7. Consequences

Substantiated harassment or discrimination will result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination of employment, regardless of the offender's seniority.

## 8. Training

All employees will receive training on this policy upon onboarding and periodically thereafter.

## 9. External Remedies

Nothing in this policy limits an employee's right to pursue a complaint with an external regulatory body or labor authority in accordance with applicable law.

## 10. Policy Review

This policy will be reviewed at least annually and updated to reflect changes in applicable law.
